About HealthHub Tracker Pro

Welcome to HealthHub Tracker Pro, a premium, privacy-first wellness and metabolic analysis suite engineered to help individuals understand and track vital physical markers entirely offline.

Our Mission

Our platform was built with a simple goal: to eliminate complex, subscription-heavy fitness tracking apps and replace them with a single, comprehensive dashboard that operates completely inside your local browser storage. We believe your biological data belongs to you—which is why your logs never touch an external cloud server.

What We Calculate

  • Body Mass Index (BMI): Standard screening limits relative to stature height.
  • Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR): Baseline daily survival caloric consumption metrics.
  • Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE): Maintenance multipliers factoring lifestyle load constraints.
  • US Navy Body Fat Estimations: Structural circumferences analysis modeling subcutaneous fat tissue density layers.
  • Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR): Critical metric tracking systemic cardiovascular and metabolic risk markers.
